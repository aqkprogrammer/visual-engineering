# Code of conduct

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions — and when representing the project elsewhere.

In short:

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new to a topic. This is a learning project; questions are contributions.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Critique content with evidence and sources.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@aqkprogrammer](https://github.com/aqkprogrammer), privately. If you need a private channel, open an issue titled "Conduct contact request" **without any details**, and the maintainer will arrange one. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer will review every report and may take any action they judge appropriate, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant: from a private correction, through a warning or a temporary ban, to a permanent ban from the project.
